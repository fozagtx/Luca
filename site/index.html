Runs on Claude Code · Codex soon · macOS 13+

  1. This is Luca.

    A live preview, a real timeline and a chat where you tell Luca what you want. Scroll to see how it works.

  2. Tell Luca what you want.

    Type or talk in plain words: “make the captions yellow”, “zoom in when I say the price”. Drop in a voiceover, footage or screenshots and Luca uses them.

  3. Watch it work.

    Luca plans the beats, builds every visual and mixes the sound, and tells you what it did in plain words. It asks before anything unusual.

  4. See every change live.

    The preview plays exactly what will export and updates within a second of every edit. Press G to grab anything in it and say what should change right there.

  5. A real editor underneath.

    Tracks, clips and your voiceover’s waveform. Trim, split and snap by hand whenever you like, or select a clip and ask Luca.

  6. Export when it’s right.

    Captions, Color and History are in the toolbar, and every change is a version you can restore. ⌘E renders an MP4 in the background.
